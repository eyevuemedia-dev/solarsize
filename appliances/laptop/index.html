How many solar panels to run a laptop?

A laptop uses about 360 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that takes about 96 W of panels and a 0.44 kWh battery for one day.

Where does the daily energy come from?

60 W × 6 h/day (assumed use). Apple's MacBook Air specs page lists a 40W Dynamic Power Adapter with 60W max for the 13-inch model, and a 53.8 Wh battery. 60 W is the adapter ceiling, not a measured average workload, so the runtime is a short estimate. Some configurations on the same page ship a 35 W dual USB-C adapter instead.
