How many solar panels to run a ceiling fan?

A ceiling fan uses about 100 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that takes about 27 W of panels and a 0.12 kWh battery for one day.

How many solar panels to run a ceiling fan?

Where does the daily energy come from?

12.5 W × 8 h/day (assumed use). ENERGY STAR lists high speed at 12.5 W, low speed at 1.6 W, and standby at 0.3 W for this 44-inch fan. The run check uses high speed. Startup watts are not published.
