How many solar panels to run a mini fridge?

A mini fridge uses about 598 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that takes about 160 W of panels and a 0.74 kWh battery for one day.

How many solar panels to run a mini fridge?

A mini fridge uses about 598 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that takes about 160 W of panels and a 0.74 kWh battery for one day.

Where does the daily energy come from?

24.9 W annual-average draw × 24 h. Danby lists 115 V, 66.4 W, and 218 kWh/year. Average draw is 218 × 1,000 ÷ 8,760 = 24.9 W. Duty cycle 0.375 is that average divided by 66.4 W, so it is an annual-average compressor fraction, not a hot-day measurement. Startup watts are not published.
