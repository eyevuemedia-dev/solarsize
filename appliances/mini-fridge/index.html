The 6 Best Mini Fridges for Solar of 2026

By the SolarSized team · Updated November 22, 2026 · Prices checked November 22, 2026

For a 120 V mini fridge, the most efficient full-height model in the ENERGY STAR list from a mainstream brand is the Avanti RMRS31X: 169 kWh a year, or about 463 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that is roughly 124 W of panels and a 0.57 kWh battery for one day. If the fridge will live on a battery (van, boat, off-grid cabin, or a power station in an outage), a 12 V compressor fridge is the better tool: Dometic rates the CFX5 45 at 1.05 Ah/h in a 90 °F room, about 302 Wh a day, and it skips the inverter, whose idle draw alone can cost a small power station more than the fridge does. From a power station the Avanti costs about 874 Wh a day; the Dometic about 302.

120 V or 12 V: which kind of mini fridge for solar?

Per cubic foot, the best 120 V compact fridges are at least as efficient as the 12 V compressor fridges: the Avanti uses about 154 Wh per cubic foot per day, the Dometic about 186. The difference is how the power gets to them.

  • On a home solar system with a big inverter that runs anyway (a grid-tied home, or an off-grid cabin with a whole-house inverter), the inverter's idle is already paid for. A 120 V fridge is cheaper, bigger and fine. Pick on kWh/yr.
  • On a power station or a small battery, the AC fridge forces the inverter to stay on 24 hours a day. At a typical 15 W idle that is 360 Wh a day before the fridge cools anything, so the Avanti costs about 874 Wh a day from the battery against 302 Wh for the Dometic on the 12 V port. A 1,000 Wh station runs the Avanti about 25 hours and the Dometic about 71 hours (usable 90%).
  • In a van, boat or truck, 12 V wins outright: no inverter, the fridge can run off the house battery, and the compressor fridges tolerate tilt and vibration (ICECO quotes 40°).

Running a full-size fridge through an outage instead? Willrun's best power station for a fridge covers runtimes, surge and which stations publish a usable idle figure. For medication, see its insulin fridge guide and follow your pharmacist's storage instructions.

Lab numbers vs a hot van

The ENERGY STAR kWh/yr figure comes from the Department of Energy test procedure, which runs the fridge closed in a 90 °F room (10 CFR 430, Subpart B, Appendix A). DOE uses that hot, closed-door test to stand in for normal use in a roughly 72 °F kitchen with the door being opened. Dometic's 1.05 Ah/h is also measured at 32 °C (90 °F) with 4 °C (39 °F) inside, so those two are roughly comparable. ICECO doesn't say what conditions its figures use.

  • Hotter than 90 °F (a closed van in the sun, a cabin loft in July), expect more. Budget 1.5× the published figure for summer, and give the fridge the ventilation clearance its manual asks for.
  • Door openings and warm groceries are only approximated by the DOE test, and the 12 V makers don't say whether their figures include any. Pre-chill food and drinks on grid or shore power before a trip.
  • Cooler rooms help a lot. A fridge in a 60 °F cabin may use well under half its rated figure. That's a bonus, not something to size on.
  • Measure yours. A plug-in watt meter on an AC fridge for a week, or the fridge's app on DC (Dometic's app logs Ah/h), beats any table. If you do, send it in with a photo.

Startup surge and inverter size for an AC mini fridge

Compact-fridge makers rarely publish start-up watts (Danby doesn't for the DCR032). The closest published numbers are for compact chest freezers with similar small compressors: Haier lists 690 W to start and 138 W running for its 3.5 cu ft HF35CM23N (see our chest freezer guide). So for an AC mini fridge on an inverter:

  • Use a pure sine inverter or power station.
  • Look for at least 600–1,000 W of rated surge that applies to motor loads, not just a resistive "boost" mode.
  • A 300 W continuous rating is plenty for the running load.

12 V compressor fridges don't need any of this. The rated DC current (8.1 A for the Dometic CFX5 45) is the most the unit is rated to draw. Wire it with the gauge and fuse the manual specifies, and set the fridge's low-voltage cutoff to suit your battery.

Questions

What is the most energy-efficient mini fridge for solar?

Among full-height 120 V compact fridges from mainstream brands in the ENERGY STAR list, the Avanti RMRS31X at 169 kWh a year (about 463 Wh a day). If it will run from a battery, a 12 V compressor fridge such as the Dometic CFX5 45 (1.05 Ah/h at 90 °F, about 302 Wh a day) uses less from the battery because it skips the inverter.

How many solar panels do I need to run a mini fridge?

About 125–150 W for an efficient 3 cu ft model at the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours (the Avanti needs 124 W, our original Danby example 160 W). In a northern December plan on 280–370 W. A 12 V compressor fridge needs about 81 W on average and 186 W in a Detroit December.

How big a battery do I need for a mini fridge?

For one day without sun, about 0.57 kWh for the Avanti on an inverter, or 0.34 kWh for the Dometic on 12 V. Double it for two cloudy days. A 100 Ah 12 V LiFePO4 battery (1.28 kWh) runs a 12 V fridge for roughly three to four days on its own.

Is a 12 V fridge better than a regular mini fridge on solar?

On a battery or power station, usually yes: no inverter, no inverter idle draw, and a smaller battery. On a home system where a big inverter runs anyway, an efficient 120 V compact fridge is cheaper and holds more.

How long will a power station run a mini fridge?

With about 90% of a 1,000 Wh station usable, roughly 25 hours for the Avanti on the AC outlet (including ~15 W of inverter idle) and about 71 hours for the Dometic CFX5 45 on the 12 V car port, on paper at 90 °F. Real runtimes depend on room temperature and how often the door opens.

Is a 1.6 cu ft cube fridge more efficient than a 3 cu ft one?

Not necessarily. In the ENERGY STAR list the popular Frigidaire EFR180 and EFR115 cubes are certified at either 158 or 206 kWh a year depending on version, while the 3.0 cu ft Avanti is 169. Check the EnergyGuide label.

Did you test these fridges?

No. We did not buy, run or meter them. The picks rest on ENERGY STAR's certified energy figures, makers' published specs, our sizing math and prices read on November 22, 2026. If you own one and have logged its Wh or Ah per day with a photo of the meter or app, you can submit it as a verified owner test.
