Where does the daily energy come from?

8.7 W × 24 h/day (assumed use). TP-Link lists typical power consumption in networked standby, with all wired ports connected and all wireless radios activated, at 8.7 W. The power adapter is 12 V, 2.5 A, which is a supply rating and is not used as the draw.
