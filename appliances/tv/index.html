How many solar panels to run a 55-inch TV?

A 55-inch TV uses about 384 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that takes about 103 W of panels and a 0.47 kWh battery for one day.

Where does the daily energy come from?

76.9 W × 5 h/day (assumed use). ENERGY STAR on-mode power for 55QNED85TU* is 76.9 W at a 54.6-inch viewable diagonal. Standby with the network disconnected is 0.30 W. On-mode power is what the station has to supply while the TV is on.
