Questions

How many solar panels to run an 8,000 Btu window AC?

An 8,000 btu window ac uses about 4,266 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that takes about 1,142 W of panels and a 5.27 kWh battery for one day.

Where does the daily energy come from?

533.3 W × 8 h/day (assumed use). ENERGY STAR lists 8,000 Btu/h, CEER 15, 115 V, variable-speed compressor, and 400 kWh/year. Rated input is 8,000 ÷ 15 = 533.3 W, which matches 400 kWh at 750 hours. Startup watts are not published.
