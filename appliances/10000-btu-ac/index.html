How many solar panels to run a 10,000 Btu window AC?

A 10,000 Btu window AC uses about 5,000 Wh a day. At the U.S. average of 4.98 sun hours that takes about 1,339 W of panels and a 6.17 kWh battery for one day.

Where does the daily energy come from?

625 W × 8 h/day (assumed use). ENERGY STAR lists model PWJV10W**# at 10,000 Btu/h, CEER 16, and 468.8 kWh/year, with a variable-speed compressor. Rated input is 10,000 ÷ 16 = 625 W. 625 × 750 ÷ 1,000 = 468.75, which rounds to the listed 468.8 kWh. Startup watts are not published.
