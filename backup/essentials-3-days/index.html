Battery size for 3 days of essential loads

Essentials (fridge, freezer, CPAP, router, TV, sump pump, laptop, fan) use about 4.1 kWh a day. 3 days of backup needs a 15.11 kWh battery; recharging daily takes about 1,092 W of panels.
