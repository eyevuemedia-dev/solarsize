Questions

How many solar panels do I need?

Divide daily watt-hours by (peak sun hours × 0.75). The 0.75 factor covers wiring, temperature, inverter and dust losses. Example: 10 kWh/day in Michigan (4.47 sun hours) needs about 2,983 W, or 8 × 400 W panels.

How big a battery do I need?

Daily watt-hours × days of backup ÷ (0.9 usable depth of discharge × 0.9 inverter efficiency).

Where do sun hours come from?

State annual averages of peak sun hours derived from NREL PVWatts v8 (NSRDB typical-year data).
